In short

  • We collect the information you give us in the audit request form (name, business, phone, email, trade, website, best time to call you back and message) or in the chatbot on the home page, and some technical data needed for the site to work.
  • We use it to call you back, prepare your audit and follow up on your request.
  • The chatbot’s answers are written by artificial intelligence. Our server does not keep the conversation: it is sent to us only if you leave your contact details, book your audit or give your feedback.
  • When our answering agent, an artificial intelligence, answers the phone, a short message first announces that the call is recorded. If you ask whether it is a person, the agent always confirms that it is virtual. The recording and its transcript are deleted after 30 days (section 4).
  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.
  • The home page and this policy use no cookies, no audience measurement, no advertising, and no font or library from another provider. Only the chatbot on the home page loads from our own server, hosted by Render. The client area, reserved for our clients, is described in section 3.
  • You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, subject to applicable legal obligations: just write to us.

1Section 1. Who we are

Agence UIO helps trade contractors stop losing customers: AI answering agent, automatic text after a missed call, control centre (appointments, Google review requests, monthly report) and custom websites. We are located in Gatineau, Quebec.

This policy explains how we handle the personal information collected on agenceuio.ca (the home page, its form, its chatbot and the client area), as well as by phone and text when our answering agent answers for us or for a client business. It was written in accordance with Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (as amended by Law 25) and the federal law (PIPEDA).

3Section 3. The information we collect

  • Audit request form: your name, your business’s name, your phone number, your email address, and if you provide them, your trade, your website address, the best time to call you back, your message, the plan you chose and the calculator scenario.
  • Home page chatbot: the messages you write in it. If you choose to give them to us: your name, your phone number or email, your business’s name, your need and the best time to call you back, or the time of the appointment you book. If you give feedback on the conversation: helpful or not, and your comment.
  • Email and Instagram: if you write to us directly, we receive your message and your contact details.
  • Calls and texts: when our answering agent answers, your number, the recording and transcript of the call, and what you say or write to it. Everything is explained in section 4.
  • Technical data: as with any website, our host (GitHub Pages) may keep technical logs (IP address, date and time, page viewed) for the security and proper operation of the site.
  • Client area: reserved for our clients, it requires a username and a password. It goes through our server hosted by Render, accounts are managed in Google Sheets, and the page loads fonts from Google Fonts (Google then receives your IP address).

How the chatbot works: your messages go through our server, hosted by Render, which sends them to OpenAI to write the answer. These answers are produced by artificial intelligence and may contain errors: our prices and terms are the ones written on the website. Our server does not keep the conversation. It is emailed to us only if you leave your contact details, book your audit or give your feedback. Your contact details and a summary of your request are then added to our list of requests (Google Sheets), and an appointment is added to our calendar (Google Calendar).

Your browser keeps the conversation in its session storage until you close the tab, so you find it again if you reload the page. This is not a cookie: it stays on your device and is not used to track you. To limit abuse, our server also keeps your IP address and the number of messages sent in memory, for 24 hours at most.

The site uses no cookies, no audience measurement tool such as Google Analytics, no targeted advertising and no profiling.

Please do not send us sensitive information in the form or in the chatbot (health, ID numbers, banking information).

4Section 4. Calls and texts

Our answering agent is an artificial intelligence that answers the phone, for Agence UIO or for a client business. At the start of each call, a short automated message gives the business’s name and announces that the call is recorded, then the agent answers. It never pretends to be a person: if you ask, it always confirms that it is a virtual answering agent.

  • What is collected: your phone number, the date and length of the call, the recording of the conversation and its written transcript, what you tell the agent (for example your name, your address, your need and the time that suits you) and the texts exchanged.
  • How it works: your voice is turned into text, an artificial intelligence writes the answer following the script approved by the business, then a synthetic voice speaks it. At the end of the call, a summary is texted to the business and to you.
  • Who has access: the business you called, for its own calls; the people at Agence UIO who install the agent and check that it follows the script, by listening to a recording or reading a transcript when needed; and our telephone and voice agent providers (section 7).
  • A person when needed: if you want to talk to someone, tell the agent. It transfers the call or takes a message so someone calls you back. The agent applies the business’s instructions, for example its service area: if you disagree with one of its answers, a person from the business can review your request.
  • Text after a missed call: if the business cannot answer, or if you hang up before speaking, an automatic text is sent to the number that called, in the business’s name, to follow up on your request. Reply STOP or ARRÊT and you will not receive any more.

We do not use your voice to recognize you (no voiceprint) or your calls to train an artificial intelligence, and your number is never used for advertising. The agent will never ask you for banking information.

If you call one of our client businesses: that business is responsible for your information, and its own privacy policy applies. Agence UIO processes it on the business’s behalf, under a written agreement, and only to provide its services (answering agent, texts, appointments, review requests). To exercise your rights, contact the business; you can also write to us and we will forward your request to it.

If you prefer not to be recorded, you can hang up and reach the business another way, for example by text or email. For Agence UIO, write to info@agenceuio.ca.

5Section 5. Why we use it

  • To call you back, prepare your free audit and, if you wish, a quote.
  • To answer your questions in the chatbot and, if you ask, book your audit in our calendar and email you the confirmation.
  • To answer your calls, take your request or appointment, text you the summary and put you through to a person when needed.
  • To check that the answering agent follows the approved script, correct its mistakes and, in case of disagreement, find what was said.
  • To improve the chatbot based on the feedback you give us.
  • To follow up on our exchanges and, if you become a client, carry out your project and bill you.
  • To protect the site against spam and abuse (for example, by limiting the number of messages per visitor).

We do not use it for any other purpose without your consent. We never sign you up for a newsletter without your agreement.

6Section 6. Your consent

By sending us the form, or by leaving your contact details in the chatbot, you consent to our using your information for the purposes described above. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us. To call you back, we need your name, your business and your phone number (or, in the chatbot, your email); the rest is optional.

On the phone, an automated message announces right at the start that the call is recorded, and the agent confirms that it is virtual if you ask. By continuing the call, you agree to it being recorded for the purposes described in section 4; otherwise, you can hang up at any time. To stop receiving our texts, reply STOP or ARRÊT.

7Section 7. Who has access

Only the people at Agence UIO who handle your request have access to it. We also use a few providers, who have access only to provide their service to us:

  • Web3Forms (Amazon Web Services servers) receives the form and emails it to us. It keeps a copy for 30 days, then deletes it automatically.
  • ImprovMX forwards emails sent to our @agenceuio.ca addresses to our inbox.
  • Google hosts our inbox (Gmail), where your requests arrive, our calendar (Google Calendar), where appointments booked in the chatbot are added, and our spreadsheets (Google Sheets): the list of requests received by the chatbot and the client accounts.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub) hosts and serves the website.
  • Render hosts our server, which runs the chatbot and the client area.
  • OpenAI writes the chatbot’s answers based on your messages.
  • Resend sends the chatbot’s emails: your request or feedback, which reaches us, and your appointment confirmation, which is sent to you.
  • Our telephone provider supplies our numbers, routes the calls and sends the texts.
  • Our voice agent platform turns voice into text, has an artificial intelligence write the answers, gives them a synthetic voice and keeps the call recording and transcript for 30 days.
  • Artificial intelligence tools, such as Anthropic’s Claude, may help us sort our emails and prepare our replies.

You can ask us at any time for the names of our telephone and voice agent providers.

We never sell, rent or trade your information.

8Section 8. Information stored outside Quebec

Some of these providers have their servers outside Quebec, including in the United States. Your information, including your calls and texts, may therefore be stored or processed there. Before entrusting information to a provider located outside Quebec, we assess whether it will be adequately protected there.

9Section 9. How long we keep it

  • Request with no follow-up: 24 months at most after our last exchange.
  • Clients: for the duration of the contract, then as long as the law requires (for example, 6 years for accounting records).
  • Chatbot conversation: deleted from your browser when you close the tab; our server does not keep it. A conversation sent to us with a request, an appointment or feedback follows the same rules as requests.
  • IP address (chatbot limits): 24 hours at most, in our server’s memory.
  • Call recording and transcript: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Request taken by phone or text: for Agence UIO, the same rules as other requests. For a client business, we keep it for that business during its contract with us; at the end of the contract, we give it its data if it asks, then delete it from our tools.

After that, we destroy it securely.

10Section 10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information: access limited to the people who need it, protected accounts and an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the Commission d’accès à l’information and the people concerned, as the law requires.

11Section 11. Your rights

Subject to applicable legal obligations, you can:

  • know what information we hold about you and get a copy, including in a commonly used technological format;
  • have it corrected if it is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • withdraw your consent or ask for it to be deleted;
  • ask us to stop disseminating it, where the law allows.

Write to the people in charge (section 2); for a call made to one of our client businesses, also see section 4. We will reply within 30 days. If our answer does not satisfy you, you can file a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.
