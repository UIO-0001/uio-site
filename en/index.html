It answers the way you would.

We write its script with you, for your trade: your services, your service area, your emergencies. It follows your rules and puts the call through to you when needed.

The lines quoted are a fictional example.

  1. It answers in your name

    After a short message saying the call is recorded, it answers in your business’s name and switches to French if the caller speaks French.

    AgentHi, bonjour! Lavoie Plumbing, how can I help you?

  2. It spots emergencies

    Water damage, a gas smell, no heat in winter: it gives the safety instructions and transfers the call to your cell right away.

  3. It takes the request and contact details

    The service, since when, the name, the number read back out loud and the address. If the address is outside your service area, it says so politely.

  4. It books

    It offers two open time slots in your calendar. With the Answering plan, it promises a callback before a set time instead.

    AgentSo: leaking water heater, at 12 rue Laval, Tuesday 9 a.m. Is that right?

  5. It sends you the summary

    A text goes to you and the customer. If the caller wants to talk to someone, or if the agent doesn’t understand twice, it transfers the call to you with a summary.

Does it understand local accents, in French and English?

It speaks French and English. Before going live, we test your script with real calls: accents, job-site noise, callers in a hurry. We only turn it on once the tests pass.

Do I keep my number?

Yes. The calls you don’t take are forwarded to the answering agent; we set up the forwarding with you at your phone provider. It can also answer all your calls; we quote that separately.

How many minutes are included?

250, 500 or 750 minutes a month depending on the plan; 250 minutes is about 80 to 190 calls. Spam and calls under 15 seconds never count. We warn you at 80%. Past your included minutes, it’s $0.39 a minute, or a block of 250 more minutes for $99 a month if you often go over.

Am I tied to a contract?

No. It’s month to month, and you can stop whenever you want. The setup fee pays for installing and testing your script. With Answering and Growth, it comes back to you as a credit on your 4th invoice; anything above that invoice ($100 with the plan alone) carries over to the 5th. This credit is never paid out in cash: if you stop before the 5th invoice, the rest isn’t refunded. The first month is money-back guaranteed, setup fee included.

I already have a website. Do I need to redo it?

No. The answering agent and the control centre work with any website. The AI chatbot can also be added to most existing websites (WordPress, Wix, Shopify, Squarespace): we install it for you, without touching your design.

Are review requests allowed by Google?

Yes, as long as there’s no filtering. All your customers get the same request, with no reward and without picking only the happy ones, as Google’s rules require.

Do you guarantee a number of customers?

No. The number of jobs depends on your calls, your prices and your service. We commit to installing the tools and making them work, and every call summary shows you what they do for you.

Do you only work with plumbers?

We specialize in trades where a missed call costs a lot: plumbing, heating and cooling, electrical, auto repair, roofing, moving. For another field, call or email us.
